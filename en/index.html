OUR VISION

From biodiversity to evidence.

At PYTHO Labs, we aim to identify promising natural compounds, understand their mechanisms, and develop safe, standardized formulations subject to scientific evaluation.

We will explore terrestrial plants and marine species, including kelp such as Macrocystis pyrifera, guided by sustainability, traceability, and regulatory compliance.

Our proposed supplements are not intended to replace medical treatments or claim curative effects without sufficient evidence and applicable authorization.

BRAND IDENTITY

Why PYTHO?

PYTHO is the name of our scientific ecosystem and token. It draws inspiration from “phyto”from the Greek word phytón (“plant”), the root of words such as phytotherapy and phytochemistry. It represents the intersection of botanical research, innovation, and applied science.

PYTHO is not related to Python, the programming language, and is not an official technology of the Python ecosystem. It is PYTHO Labs’ own brand identity.

TRANSPARENCY

Frequently asked questions

Does the PYTHO token price determine economic participation?

The proposed model separates the token’s market price from any distributed economic results. The formula, rights, and percentage must be specified in legal documentation. The token’s market price may fluctuate independently.

Will the supplements replace medication?

No. PYTHO Labs intends to study supplements for wellness or as potential complements, without replacing medical treatments or claiming unproven therapeutic properties.

When is the first product expected?

The project targets its first product in the second year of funding, subject to scientific results, toxicology, validation, manufacturing, and required approvals.

Can I buy PYTHO tokens now?

No. This is a conceptual project website. Tokenization, participant rights, and any issuance mechanisms require technical and legal definition first.
